
# 1. Responsible AI

## What is Responsible AI?

**Responsible AI** means designing and using AI in a way that is 
safe, fair, transparent, secure, inclusive, and accountable.

Microsoft commonly describes six key principles: 

- **Fairness**
- **Reliability & Safety**
- **Privacy & Security**
- **Inclusiveness**
- **Transparency**
- **Accountability**
